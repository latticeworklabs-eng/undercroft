# Paul Graham

Programmer, essayist, and investor. Co-founded Viaweb (sold to Yahoo, 1998) and Y Combinator (2005); writes the long-running essay series at paulgraham.com.

## From [[2026-05-04-how-to-do-great-work]]

- Wrote the essay (2023-07) as an explicit attempt to compute the *intersection* of great-work techniques across fields — "a guide that could be used by someone working in any field."
- Prescribed a four-step recipe (field → frontier → gaps → explore), powered by curiosity rather than planning — see [[curiosity-as-compass]].
- Modeled careers as exponential processes; his warning about the flat early stretch of the curve anchors [[work-that-compounds]].
- Stated audience: the very ambitious — the recipe "assumes you're very ambitious."
