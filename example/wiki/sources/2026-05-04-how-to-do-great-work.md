# How to Do Great Work

## TL;DR

- [[paul-graham]]'s computed intersection of great-work techniques across all fields — the result is a recipe with a definite shape, "not just a point labelled 'work hard.'"
- The core loop: "Four steps: choose a field, learn enough to get to the frontier, notice gaps, explore promising ones."
- The selection problem is solved by doing, not planning — aptitude, interest, and scope reveal themselves only through work.
- Motivation, ranked: curiosity above delight above the desire to impress → [[curiosity-as-compass]].
- Great work rides exponential processes that feel flat early → [[work-that-compounds]].

## Key claims

1. **(on choosing)** The work you choose needs three qualities: "it has to be something you have a natural aptitude for, that you have a deep interest in, and that offers scope to do great work." None of the three is reliably visible in advance: "The way to figure out what to work on is by working."
2. **(the recipe)** "Four steps: choose a field, learn enough to get to the frontier, notice gaps, explore promising ones." Gaps only become visible from the frontier — noticing them means taking anomalies seriously instead of explaining them away.
3. **(on motives)** "The three most powerful motives are curiosity, delight, and the desire to do something impressive." Graham treated curiosity as the strongest and most durable — it both selects the field and powers the work.
4. **(on compounding)** Great work disproportionately comes from processes with compounding returns; learning and audience are his canonical examples. People quit compounding work precisely because "the curve feels flat in the beginning."
5. **(scope condition)** The recipe explicitly "assumes you're very ambitious" — it optimizes for the tail of outcomes, not the average career.

## Open questions

- Graham treats curiosity as a *reliable* compass for problem selection. The strongest counter-position on record is importance-first selection — Hamming's "You and Your Research" (1986). Queue it as a deliberate counter-source.
- Is "scope to do great work" detectable from outside a field, or only from the frontier? Candidate test cases: fields Graham himself moved between (painting → Lisp → essays).
